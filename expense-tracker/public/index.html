How your data is protected

  • Credentials never touch this app. Bank sign-in happens inside Plaid's hosted widget; this server only receives a scoped, revocable access token.
  • Encryption at rest. Access tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being written to the local database.
  • Hardened sessions. Session cookies are httpOnly, SameSite=Strict, and stored server-side only as SHA-256 hashes with 12-hour expiry.
  • Brute-force protection. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited; passwords are hashed with bcrypt (cost 12).
  • CSRF & clickjacking defenses. State-changing requests require a custom header; strict Content-Security-Policy and frame-ancestors 'none'.
  • Local-first. Your data stays in a local SQLite database on your machine — nothing is sent to third parties except Plaid for account syncing.
